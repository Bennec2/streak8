How to Play

Guess if the next card is Higher or Lower than the current one.

  • Survive the first 4 cards (Qualifier) to enter the Jackpot Run.
  • In the Qualifier, a wrong guess lets you Continue by halving the jackpot.
  • In the Jackpot Run, any wrong guess ends the hand — and costs you the current tier.
  • You can Cash Out safely from the 6th card onward.
  • A tie (push) halves the jackpot but you advance.

Tip: swap a bad base card for $100 before your first guess. Aces can't go higher, 2s can't go lower.
