# Open Questions for Playtesting

Consolidated tuning questions from across the design docs:

1. Round length: is 8 cards right, or should a future mode offer shorter or
   longer boards?
2. Qualifier payouts: keep the qualifier payout-free, or add small consolation
   payouts?
3. Push penalty: is halving the jackpot on ties too punishing vs −25%?
4. Cash-out availability: lock the 7th/8th cards, or keep cash-out open
   throughout the run?
5. Continue scaling: should repeated qualifier continues cost more than 50%?
6. Swap pricing: flat $100, or scaled to the jackpot?
7. Jackpot response: should cash-outs also move the jackpot range?
8. Bankroll resets: settings reset, season resets, or never?
9. Suit relevance: side bets, or cosmetic forever?
10. Auto-restart: keep the forced 2-second breather, or make it skippable?
11. Negative balance: recovery mechanic, or informational only?
12. Visual identity: does neon-casino match the App Store positioning?
13. Future modes: timed streaks, daily jackpot, or pure solo forever?
